How it works

  • One household, many learners

    A household has guardians, learner profiles for children, and optionally an adult profile for self-directed study.

  • Build courses, by hand or with a draft

    Guardians can write a course themselves or ask for an AI-assisted draft. A draft stays a draft until a guardian reviews and publishes it.

  • Lessons, quizzes and assignments

    Learners work through lessons, take quizzes and submit assignments. Guardians review the work and grade it.
